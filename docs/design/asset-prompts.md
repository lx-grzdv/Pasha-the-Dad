# ImageGen: материалы новой версии

Все три изображения созданы встроенным ImageGen, без CLI и внешних API.

## Целевой кадр — `pasha-3d-target.png`

Input: screenshot of the original local Phaser game.

Create a target in-engine screenshot for the next version of the attached Russian browser game Pasha the D[e]ad. Keep the premise: a bearded dad with dark hair, lime green T-shirt, shorts and pink slippers holding a baby in a pink wrap, little daughter with dark hair and mustard yellow dress next to him, household and work tasks flying toward him. Transform this flat pixel game into a beautiful actual feasible Three.js stylized 3D toy-diorama browser game, NOT a painting or cinematic. Wide 16:9 composition. Camera looking down at a moderate angle into a cozy cutaway apartment on a dark desaturated teal background, two walls, warm cream plaster, golden oak plank floor, teal sofa on left, mustard cushions, designer's desk and monitor on right rear, big warm window, pot plants, framed graphic prints, little books and toys. Center large clearly readable chunky rounded dad and daughter on circular cream rug. Very polished bevelled geometry, clay-like matte materials, warm sunlight, soft shadows, subtle ambient occlusion. 3D objects and small floating task cards approach center. Restrained premium dark olive translucent interface, lime accent, compact top header with title 'ПАША ПРОТИВ ДНЯ', a small '03:00' timer, 5 compact status bars on left, 3 bottom key action buttons labelled Q, E, R. Scene dominates screen and characters must be clearly visible. No unrealistic photorealism, no flat placeholders. All gameplay remains in a frontal plane, camera doesn't obscure character limbs. This image is our visual target only.

## Дерево — `public/textures/oak-albedo.png`

Use case: game material texture. Create a single seamless tileable square albedo texture of beautiful warm honey oak wood grain for a cozy stylized 3D toy apartment wooden floor. Orthographic straight-on flat scan, no perspective, no lighting, no baked shadows, no objects, no text, no border. Fine painterly but physically plausible wood fibers with subtle natural variations and knots. Grain runs horizontally, no large plank divisions (plank seams will be modeled separately). Warm pale golden tan, restrained contrast, matte, high-quality miniature clay game visual. 1024 by 1024 texture.

## Ткань — `public/textures/linen-albedo.png`

Game material texture, a perfectly flat orthographic scan of a seamless tileable fine woven linen upholstery fabric in ivory/off-white. One uniform fabric, no folds, no shadows, no perspective, no objects, no text, no borders or seams. Visible fine interwoven natural yarn fibers, understated irregularity, gentle soft contrast, neutral almost white albedo suitable for tinting teal or mustard in a 3D renderer. Stylized high quality cozy miniature game furniture surface. 1024 square.
